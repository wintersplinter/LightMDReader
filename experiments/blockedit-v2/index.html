Block Edit v2

Click any block to edit it as Markdown. Click another block, or press Esc, to render it again. ↑/↓ at the edge of a block move to the neighbour. A blank line inside a block splits it. Backspace at the very start merges it into the block above. Ctrl+Enter opens a new block below. Dimmed blocks are definitions — real lines with no rendered output. A hollow red ring in the left margin means the block carries a hidden comment. The footnote list at the end is generated: click an entry to jump to its definition.
